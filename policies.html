Environmental Policy

We, Al-Rahim Textile Industries, manufacturer of terry products, strive to create a clean environment in all our activities, products, and services by committing ourselves to:

  • Conserve natural resources through reuse, recycle, and reduce
  • Prevent pollution through effective control measures
  • Comply with all applicable and legal requirements
  • Work towards continual improvements in our environmental performance and periodically review the established system
  • Communicate the policy to all company staff and other interested parties

Our Goals

  • Reduce the quantity of polluting substances discharged into the environment
  • Increase the use of environmentally acceptable materials
  • Use modern equipment and technology in place of those considered harmful
  • Minimize the impact of all our activities on our surroundings
  • Recycle and reuse products wherever possible

We accept the commitment of every member of Al-Rahim Textile Industries along with vendors and suppliers to this policy, in action and intent.

Social Policy

At Al-Rahim Textile Industries (ARTI) we are committed to maintaining a Social Management System based on Social, Ethical BSCI, ETI, and customer Codes of Conduct including legal requirements and social obligations towards employees and customers.

  • We are committed to paying our employees at par or above legitimate standards with all benefits under legal, social, and ethical norms in a conducive and safe working environment
  • We encourage freedom of association and employee participation at all levels, supporting their continual improvement
  • We condemn bonded/forced labor, child labor, and discrimination on any ground
  • We discourage all forms of discrimination during employment including with respect to color, caste, gender, or disability at the time of appointment, wage determination, promotion, training, and retirement
  • We do not support the use of corporal punishment, mental or physical coercion, or verbal abuse
  • We ensure full obligation to national laws, local rules, and regulations

ARTI plays a significant role in the development of society through corporate social responsibility practices. ARTI is committed to developing its suppliers and service providers to meet legal and ethical social requirements.
