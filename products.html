Innovation

Specialty & Premium Lines

Innovation-driven product lines that deliver differentiated value for retail buyers and end consumers.

Bamboo

Softer than cotton with a texture similar to cashmere and silk. Twice as absorbent and takes on dye better. Naturally antimicrobial a top pick for hospitality and domestic use. Eco-friendly and biodegradable.

Zero Twist

Cotton fiber remains hollow making towels softer, more absorbent, more durable, and less prone to shrinking or pilling. Retains color better even after repeated washing. Wound with PVA during weaving.

Modal

Made from biodegradable fiber that is 50% more hygroscopic than cotton. More resistant to shrinkage and fading. Softness, durability, and consistency similar to bamboo fabrics.

Bleach Mate

Colors stay bright and fresh even when using bleach in detergents. No more worrying about fading engineered for long-lasting vibrancy through everyday washing.

Colors for Life

Tired of changing towels after the first few washes? Our Colors for Life range ensures towels stay looking fresh and new even after multiple washes.

Supima

Combed with fine brushes to remove impurities and short fibers resulting in longer, straighter, more lustrous cotton that adds premium value over traditional ring-spun towels.
