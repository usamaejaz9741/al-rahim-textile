Our Leadership Team

Al-Rahim is a family-owned business with a passion to remain matchless in the world of home textiles through sustainable business development and growth.

Abdul Rahim Saya

Chairman

Visionary founder who established Al-Rahim Textile Industries in 1991, building it into one of Pakistan's largest home textile manufacturers and exporters.

Faisal Rahim Saya

Chief Executive Officer

Brings strategic business vision to the company, driving growth in global markets and strengthening long-term partnerships with international retail brands.

Shehzad Rahim Saya

A textile graduate who imparts valuable technical knowhow and skills to the business. Under his leadership, Al-Rahim continuously produces world-class quality products for valued customers.
