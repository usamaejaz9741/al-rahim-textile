Information We Collect

Al-Rahim Textile Industries collects information you voluntarily provide through our website forms, including your name, company name, email address, phone number, and any details you include in your inquiry or message.

We may also collect non-personal information automatically, such as browser type, operating system, and pages visited, to improve our website experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries, quotation requests, and sampling requests
  • To communicate about products, services, and business opportunities
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal obligations

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website or conducting our business, provided they agree to keep this information confidential.

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect some website functionality.
