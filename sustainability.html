Raw Materials

Sustainable Sourcing

Currently at 40% sustainable materials with a target of 60%. We actively use recycled cotton and polyester certified under GRS (Global Recycled Standard) and RCS (Recycled Claim Standard).

Our commitment to sustainable sourcing extends across our entire product portfolio from organic cotton towels to recycled fiber blends ensuring that environmental responsibility is built into every product we manufacture.

Energy

Renewable Energy Leadership

62% of our energy comes from renewable sources through green energy sourcing. We are actively installing wind turbines with a target of 40% renewable energy contribution from on-site generation.

Our independent power generation system ensures uninterrupted production while we transition toward a cleaner energy mix reducing our carbon footprint without compromising manufacturing capacity.

Renewable Energy via Green Sourcing

Targeting 7.5 MW Wind Power (2026), 7.5 MW Solar Energy (2026), and 15-ton Biomass (2025) to increase on-site renewable generation.

Water Management

Conserving Every Drop

Our Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP) enables us to reuse up to 75% of water from production. We maintain zero hazardous discharge all waste water is treated and recycled before release.

We also recycle hot water and hot air from production processes, reducing both energy consumption and environmental impact across our manufacturing operations.

Environmental Policy

We, Al-Rahim Textile Industries, manufacturer of terry products, strive to create a clean environment in all our activities, products, and services by committing ourselves to:

  • Conserve natural resources through reuse, recycle, and reduce
  • Prevent pollution through effective control measures
  • Comply with all applicable legal requirements
  • Work towards continual improvements in our environmental performance
  • Communicate the policy to all company staff and interested parties

Our Goals:

  • Reduce the quantity of polluting substances discharged into the environment
  • Increase the use of environmentally acceptable materials
  • Use modern equipment and technology in place of those considered harmful
  • Minimize the impact of all activities on our surroundings
  • Recycle and reuse products wherever possible
